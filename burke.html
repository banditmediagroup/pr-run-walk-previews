About the Burke store

A Burke running store since 2003

Burke opened in 2003, a few months after our very first store in Ashburn, which makes it the second-oldest =PR= Run & Walk. Neighbors here have been lacing up with us for more than two decades, and the team still lives in the community it serves.

Come in with a marathon goal, a nagging heel, a first-ever 5K, or a pair of walking shoes that finally need replacing. We’ll listen first, then show you what fits. If nothing on the wall is right for your feet, we’ll tell you that too.

We were named the 2024 Running Store of the Year by The Running Event, and we try to earn it again every time someone walks through the door.

Shoe fitting in Burke, VA

The =PR= fit process

Every pair on our wall is one we’d run or walk in ourselves. The trick is matching your feet, your stride, and your miles to the pair that earns its keep. Here’s how a fitting works at the Burke store.

See the full fit process
1
5 minutes

The conversation

What you’re training for, what’s been bothering you, and what you wear when you’re not running. We listen first, so every recommendation is built around you.

2
Free

3D foot scan

Length, width, arch height, and pressure points, captured in under a minute. We keep the data on file, so your next visit picks up where this one left off.

3
On the treadmill

Gait analysis

A short walk or jog, a slow-motion replay, and a plain-English explanation of how your foot lands and rolls. You leave with a shortlist that makes sense for how you move.

4
Your call

Try and compare

The best way to choose a shoe is to feel the difference. You’ll try two or three pairs, sometimes more, until one feels right for your feet, goals, and lifestyle.

Running in Burke

Where to run near Burke

Burke sits at the crossroads of some of Northern Virginia’s best runs and walks. Here are three local favorites, and what to know about the ground under your shoes.

Gravel and dirt

Burke Lake Park loop

A loop of nearly five miles around the lake on gravel and packed dirt. Road shoes handle it fine on dry days. After a wet stretch, a trail shoe with more grip (the bumpy tread on the bottom) keeps you steady.

Mixed surfaces

Lake Accotink Park loop

Roughly a four-mile loop mixing gravel, dirt, and paved sections, with a connection to the Cross County Trail. A cushioned everyday trainer is a comfortable match.

Stream valleys

Cross County Trail

The Gerry Connolly Cross County Trail follows stream valleys for more than 40 miles, so you can stack up long, quiet miles away from traffic.

Distances vary a little by source and by the route you pick, so treat them as ballparks. Ask the Burke team which route fits your pace and the shoes you’re wearing.

Community

Run and walk with your neighbors

The Burke store has long been a meeting point for local runs and a pickup spot for community race packets. Whether you’re starting from the couch or training for a half marathon, there’s a group and a start line for you.
