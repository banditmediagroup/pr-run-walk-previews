    At every store

    The same expert fit, wherever you walk in

    Whichever store you choose, you’ll get the =PR= fit process: a conversation about your goals, a 3D foot scan, gait analysis, and a side-by-side try-on.

    01

    Free 3D foot scan

    Length, width, arch height, and pressure points, captured in under a minute and kept on file for your next visit.

    02

    Gait analysis

    A short walk or jog on the treadmill and a plain-English explanation of how your foot lands and rolls.

    03

    Walk-ins welcome

    No appointment needed, and a fitting takes about 20 minutes on average. We fit runners, walkers, and kids.

    Location FAQs

    There are nine stores: eight in Northern Virginia (Arlington, Burke, Leesburg, One Loudoun in Ashburn, Reston, South Riding, Springfield, and Vienna) and one in Midlothian, near Richmond.

    Most stores are open Monday Noon to 8pm, Tuesday through Friday 11am to 8pm, Saturday 10am to 6pm, and Sunday Noon to 5pm. One Loudoun, Reston, Vienna, and Midlothian open at 10am Tuesday through Friday, and Midlothian opens at 10am on Mondays. Each store card above shows exact hours.

    No. Walk-ins are welcome at every =PR= store. A fitting takes about 20 minutes on average.

    Every fitting includes a conversation about your goals, a 3D foot scan, gait analysis, and a side-by-side try-on. It’s free, and if nothing in the store is right for your feet, we’ll say so.

    Trail is a specialty at Leesburg, One Loudoun, Reston, South Riding, Springfield, and Midlothian.

    Birkenstock is a specialty at Arlington, One Loudoun, Reston, and South Riding.
